    Race rules
    • Everyone gets the same questions in the same order and goes at their own pace.
    • After each answer, right or wrong, you wait 3 s before you can submit again (you can type meanwhile).
    • A correct answer brings up your next question right away.
    • Up to 3 hints per question, each adding 10 s to your wait. You can't take another hint until the last one's 10 s is up. After 30 s on a question, the next hint is free.
    • "First hint shown": hint 1 is up on every question for free.
    • First to finish wins; everyone else is ranked by how far they got.
    Scoring
    • Answers unlock after 3 s (you can type before then).
    • Correct within 0.5 s of unlock: 1000, then it slides down to 500 by the time cap.
    • Each wrong answer: -200 from your correct score.
    • 2nd correct: -100. 3rd correct: -300. Minimum 100 for a correct answer.
    • The question ends when half the room (rounded up) is correct.
